# Employee Performance & Productivity Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Employee Performance & Productivity Analytics** project is an HR Analytics portfolio project designed to analyze employee performance, productivity, goal achievement, quality, engagement, training, attendance, overtime, recognition, and development requirements.

The project uses a **synthetic dataset of 180 employees across 8 departments** and demonstrates the complete analytics workflow from data preparation and SQL analysis to dashboard development.

The objective is to provide HR teams and managers with a structured view of workforce performance and identify areas that may require further review, development, training, or workforce planning.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze employee performance across departments and job roles.
- Measure employee productivity using multiple performance indicators.
- Track goal achievement and task completion.
- Analyze quality and stakeholder feedback.
- Understand employee engagement and job satisfaction.
- Analyze training and development requirements.
- Monitor attendance and absence patterns.
- Analyze overtime and workload patterns.
- Identify performance trends.
- Analyze recognition and promotion patterns.
- Compare performance across departments, locations, and job levels.
- Develop SQL queries from basic to advanced levels.
- Create dashboard-ready HR KPIs.
- Present HR insights through Excel and Power BI.

---

## 🏢 Business Scenario

Organizations collect large amounts of employee data related to performance, productivity, attendance, training, engagement, and development.

However, HR teams often need a consolidated analytical view to answer questions such as:

- Which departments have higher average performance?
- Which departments have higher productivity?
- Are employees achieving their assigned goals?
- Which employees or groups require development support?
- What are the employee engagement patterns?
- Which departments have higher overtime?
- How does quality compare with productivity?
- What performance trends are visible across employees?

This project addresses these questions using SQL analysis and dashboard-based reporting.
